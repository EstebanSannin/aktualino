A tiny two-repo Uptane/TUF OTA client for the ESP32 & ESP32-S3. version {{VERSION}}

Plug your board in over USB and click Connect. The flasher auto-detects an ESP32 or ESP32-S3. These are the no-secrets public images: after flashing, the board opens its own Wi-Fi setup portal — nothing is baked in.

Aktualino — standard

The core two-repo OTA client + SoftAP setup portal. Start here.

Your browser can't flash — use desktop Chrome, Edge, or Opera. You must allow serial access to flash.

Aktualino — with Berry script secondary

Adds the optional Berry script secondary: deploy small scripts over the air as their own two-repo-verified Uptane target. Needs a bundle assigned to do anything.

Your browser can't flash — use desktop Chrome, Edge, or Opera. You must allow serial access to flash.
After flashing — set it up:
  1. On a phone or laptop, join the open Wi-Fi aktualino-<xxxxxx> the board broadcasts.
  2. The captive setup page opens automatically (or browse http://192.168.4.1/).
  3. Pick your Wi-Fi and paste a Torizon Cloud provisioning credential; the board enrolls itself.
See docs/provisioning.md for the full flow.

Prefer the command line? Every release also ships the raw .bin images + SHA256SUMS on the releases page, flashable with esptool.py (see the release notes).

Aktualino is Apache-2.0. Flashing runs entirely in your browser over WebSerial — the firmware never leaves your machine except onto your board. · Source on GitHub
